## 2. Competitive gaps (real competitors, verified via web research)

**AgileNDT** (agilendt.com) — cloud-based since 2011, the most directly comparable specialist. Modular: reporting, work requests, customer portal, dashboards, plus certs/quotes/billing/inventory/technique-sheets. Positioning line: *"the best NDT software fit depends on workflow, not feature count."* No public pricing (dedicated pricing page exists but gated behind demo request). Notably does **not** market offline capture or digital signatures prominently — a gap Atlantis and others exploit.

**FloodlightSoft** (floodlightsoft.com) — the sharpest positioning in the niche: *"Deliver Same-Day Inspection Reports. Automatically."* Full offline mobile capture, auto-report generation, digital signatures + approval workflow, template library across all 6 core methods (UT/RT/MT/PT/VT/ET), explicit compliance list (API 510/570/653, ASME Section V & B31.3, AWS D1.1, ASTM, ISO 9712, ASNT), plus quote-to-cash integration (proposal → invoice). Publishes a **14-day free trial** — the only named competitor with a frictionless self-serve entry point. This is the closest analog to Atlantis Reporting Software and the one to benchmark hardest against.

**InspectO** (via spainnovision.com) — *"Intelligent NDT Management from Site to Report."* Offline capture with AI-powered OCR (claims 70% less manual entry — a differentiated, quantified claim Atlantis doesn't currently match), ERP integration, multilingual (Arabic/French/English), and explicitly targets **India, UAE, USA** — directly overlapping Atlantis's current best-performing geography (Middle East) and the USA priority tier.

**Equipment-tied/enterprise adjacents** (harder to displace, not primarily SEO competitors on this exact query): Evident (OmniScan/WeldSight/OmniPC), Eddyfi (Capture/Magnifi), Waygate Technologies/Baker Hughes (Capture/Mentor/DMS GO+), MISTRAS PCMS, Eclipse Scientific (ESBeamTool, InspectionBank), Zertify, NDTbase, Proceq CYLINK, DigiView, ComplianceQuest. These win on brand trust and hardware bundling, not content marketing.

**How they win visibility:** all three named specialists (AgileNDT, FloodlightSoft, InspectO) run dedicated comparison/feature pages with concrete, specific claims (quantified time savings, named compliance codes, named integration targets) rather than generic bullet lists — exactly what Atlantis's own `/ndt-reporting-software-comparison` page lacks (hence its position-64 invisibility). Trade press (OnestopNDT) and directory-style aggregators (SourceForge, wifitalents, Capterra, SoftwareSuggest) also occupy real page-1 real estate — this category rewards being *listed and named* in third-party roundups, not just owned-domain content.

**Notable finding — an internal asset, not a competitor:** `ndt-software-solutions.vercel.app` (ranking on page 1 for "best NDT reporting software 2026") is confirmed to be **Atlantis's own satellite site** (heavily self-linking, mentions "NDTConnect Platform"), currently thin — no real competitor names, no pricing, no feature matrix despite the promising title. This is a quick-win asset to flesh out rather than a threat.

## 3. Prioritized recommendations

**1. Fix the comparison page before building anything new (highest impact, lowest effort).** `/ndt-reporting-software-comparison` gets 256 impressions at position 64 doing nothing but generic bullets. Rewrite it as a real named comparison — Atlantis vs. AgileNDT vs. FloodlightSoft vs. InspectO — with a feature/compliance matrix (offline capture, digital signatures, OCR, ERP integration, named standards). The one data point in hand (`compare-atlantis-reporting-vs-hexagon-ali-2026` ranking position 2.3 on almost no content investment) says named-competitor comparisons are the highest-leverage content type in this niche. Also strengthen the `ndt-software-solutions.vercel.app` satellite the same way — it already has page-1 real estate for the exact target query.

**2. Close the USA CTR gap, don't chase more USA volume yet.** USA already gets 215 impressions/90d and converts zero clicks — a title/meta/positioning problem, not a visibility problem. Before adding any new USA content, rewrite the flagship page's title/meta and above-fold copy to match how FloodlightSoft and InspectO win (specific, quantified: "same-day reports," "70% less manual entry," named ASME/API sections) instead of generic "affordable, accessible, customizable" language. This is a same-week fix.

**3. Stop investing in new US/Canada reporting city pages; the existing 20+ are dark.** Houston, Denver, Tulsa, Beaumont, Mobile, St. Louis, Cleveland, Cincinnati, Nashville, Louisville, Kansas City, Minneapolis, Milwaukee, Charlotte, Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Fort McMurray, Halifax all show 0-4 impressions in 90 days. This matches the site's known pSEO-saturation pattern (116 of ~5,000 pages drive 80% of clicks). Do not add more reporting city permutations under `ALLOW_PERMUTATION_GEN`; the format isn't working for this product in this geography. Redirect that effort into #1 and #4.

**4. Use the 20 new blog posts as the USA wedge, and track them separately.** Since blog content (not city pages) is the newly-added lever and this niche rewards specific/quantified claims, prioritize promoting/interlinking the posts most aligned with what's winning for competitors: offline field capture, ASME Section V compliance, digital signatures — exactly the FloodlightSoft/InspectO playbook. Pull a follow-up GSC check on these specific 20 URLs in ~4-6 weeks (they're too new to show in this 90-day window) rather than assuming the product-family numbers above will hold.

**5. Positioning call: keep Reporting as a distinct page, but market it as ERP-native, not ERP-optional.** The scale gap is stark — 58 reporting routes vs. 1,137 ERP routes (762 of them `/ndt-erp-*`) — roughly 15-20x. Standalone "NDT reporting software" search demand is real but small (confirmed above), and every named specialist competitor (AgileNDT, FloodlightSoft, InspectO) already bundles reporting with certs/billing/inventory/CRM-adjacent modules — nobody is winning by staying reporting-only. Recommendation: **do not fold the dedicated page into ERP** (it already has the better-performing content and the SERP presence), but tighten cross-linking so Reporting content explicitly sells "module of the ERP" for the mid-market buyer while the standalone page still captures direct "NDT reporting software" intent. This avoids diluting the page that's actually working (position ~10.9, 2,411 impr/90d) while stopping the resource split that's produced 762 thin ERP city pages against 58 mostly-dark reporting ones.

**6. Geo sequencing for any new investment (USA-first per standing rule):** USA copy/CTR fix → GBR (already position 21-37, has real impressions, needs on-page push) → Canada/Australia (best positions on record — 2.0 and 4.6 — but near-zero volume, so low priority for new pages, high priority for keeping what already ranks well) → India/Middle East (currently the *only* tier with real traffic, via the city pages — maintain, don't expand, since InspectO already dominates positioning there with a multilingual, region-specific product).
