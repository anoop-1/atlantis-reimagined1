NDT training online: what you can complete from anywhere

Online NDT training covers the theory, the formal classroom hours and the written-exam preparation for ASNT SNT-TC-1A Level I, Level II and Level III. At Atlantis NDT it is taught live online by an ASNT Level III, with recorded replays and study material between sessions. The parts that cannot be done online are the supervised on-the-job experience, the vision test and the practical examination on real specimens. We deliver the hands-on training and practical exam onsite at the employer's facility. That split is the core of how a legitimate online NDT programme works, and the rest of this page explains it method by method.

How the online delivery works

Live online sessions, led by an ASNT Level III

Courses run as scheduled live online sessions led by Anoop Rayavarapu, ASNT NDT Level III and founder of Atlantis NDT. Trainees ask questions in real time, work through calculations together and review code extracts on screen. Sessions are recorded, so a technician pulled onto a night shift or a turnaround can catch up without losing the thread.

Study material and practice questions between sessions

Between live sessions, trainees work through reading assignments, worked examples and practice questions in the style of the general and specific exams. Free examples are on our blog, including UT Level II practice questions and the MT, PT, RT, VT and ET mock exams.

Simulator practice with Practical NDT

Theory sticks better when trainees can see it. Practical NDT, the first-ever online practical NDT simulation portal, lets trainees practise 12 methods in 5 virtual environments (an NDT lab, a welding workshop, an aircraft hangar, an oil and gas site and a storage tank) and get scored feedback. It is used to rehearse setup, scanning and interpretation between sessions. It is practice only. It does not replace hands-on training or the practical exam on real specimens.

Documented hours

Each trainee's attendance and hours are recorded per method and level, so the employer's Level III can count them as formal training under the written practice. Before enrolling, check that your employer's written practice permits online formal training. Most do, and if yours does not yet, it can be amended.

Who online NDT training suits

Employers with distributed crews. Technicians across several sites or states attend the same live sessions without travel, and the practical is run as a concentrated onsite block. Programmes for companies are covered on corporate NDT training.

Working technicians moving from Level I to Level II. Evening and recorded sessions fit around shift work. Experience continues to build on the job while the theory is completed.

Level II technicians preparing for Level III. The Level III exams are entirely written, so preparation works well online.

Career starters. Online theory is a legitimate start, but the experience and certification come from an employer. Our guide to becoming an NDT technician and NDT schools guide explain the route honestly.

Level I, Level II and Level III online: what changes at each level

Level I online is about fundamentals: how the method works, what the equipment does, how to follow a procedure and record results. The online format suits it well because the concepts are new and trainees benefit from repeating recorded sessions. The limitation is obvious. A Level I trainee has usually touched very little equipment, so simulator practice and early supervised work matter more at this stage than at any other.

Level II online adds technique selection, calibration to a code, interpretation and evaluation against acceptance criteria, and the ability to guide Level I technicians. The theory is heavier and more mathematical for UT, RT and ET, so live sessions spend more time on worked problems. Trainees at this level already have field experience, which makes online discussion richer. They bring real calibration problems and real indications to class.

Level III online is exam preparation for the ASNT Basic and method exams, plus the procedure-writing and programme knowledge a Level III needs. Nothing in the ASNT Level III exams is hands-on, so the whole preparation can be online. The simulator is used only as a method refresher for candidates who have been away from the equipment.

How an employer programme runs with online theory

  1. Scope. The employer lists the methods, levels and number of technicians, and confirms that its written practice allows online formal training. If it does not, the Level III amends it.
  2. Online theory. Technicians attend the live sessions from their sites. Attendance and hours are recorded per person, per method and per level.
  3. Simulator practice. Technicians practise setup and interpretation on Practical NDT between sessions, and the instructor reviews common mistakes in class.
  4. Onsite practical block. An ASNT Level III delivers hands-on training at the employer's facility, on the employer's equipment and specimens, scheduled around production or shutdowns.
  5. Examinations. General, specific and practical exams are administered and graded to the written practice, by the employer's Level III or by Atlantis as the outsourced Level III.
  6. Certification. The employer certifies each technician once experience and vision requirements are met, and files the training and exam records for audit.

This sequence keeps technicians productive during the classroom phase and concentrates the hands-on work where it belongs. For multi-site companies it also means every technician is taught the same material by the same Level III, which makes the written practice easier to audit. See SNT-TC-1A training and certification for the employer-side requirements in more detail.

Online versus in-person classroom: the honest trade-offs

Online theory removes travel, lets technicians keep working, and makes recorded sessions available for review, which helps with the heavier UT, RT and ET material. It also makes it easier to train small numbers of people at different sites at the same time. What it loses is the chance to pass equipment around the room during theory. That is why simulator practice runs alongside the sessions and why the onsite practical block is not optional. Trainees who struggle with self-discipline do better in live sessions than with self-study alone, so we keep the live element central rather than selling recorded-only access. What you need to take part

  • A computer with a reliable internet connection, a camera and a microphone for live sessions. A tablet works for replays.
  • A quiet place for the live sessions. Site offices work fine.
  • A scientific calculator for UT, RT and ET courses.
  • Access to the relevant code or standard extracts your employer works to, for example ASME Section V, which we reference in class.
  • An employer contact (usually the Level III or QA manager) if the hours are to count under a written practice.

Online NDT training: more questions answered

Is online NDT training accepted under SNT-TC-1A?

Yes, as formal training, when the employer's written practice allows it and the hours are documented. The employer still controls experience, exams and certification.

Which NDT methods can I study online?

UT, PAUT, TOFD, RT, MT, PT, VT and ET theory for Level I and II, and Level III exam preparation for the Basic and the UT, RT, MT, PT, VT and ET method exams.

Can the practical exam be done online?

No. The practical is performed with real equipment on real specimens. Atlantis delivers it onsite at the employer's facility under an ASNT Level III.

Are the sessions live or self-paced?

Live online sessions with recorded replays, plus self-study material and practice questions between sessions.

Who teaches the online courses?

Anoop Rayavarapu, ASNT NDT Level III and founder of Atlantis NDT, leads the training.

Do I get a certificate at the end?

You receive documented training records per method and level. NDT certification itself is issued by your employer under its written practice once experience, vision and exam requirements are met.

Can PAUT and TOFD be learned online?

The theory can: array physics, focal laws, scan planning, diffraction and sizing. Encoded scanning and analysis on real welds is done in the onsite practical. Both require UT Level II first.

Can Level III preparation be done fully online?

Yes. The ASNT Level III Basic and method exams are written, computer-based exams, so all the preparation can be online.

Is radiography training fully online?

RT theory is online. Radiation safety training and any radiographer licensing follow state or NRC requirements, and interpretation practice on real radiographs is part of the onsite element.

What does online NDT training cost?

We quote on request. The quote depends on method, level, group size and whether onsite practical training is included, and we return it within one business day.

Where does the training take place?

Theory is delivered live online. Practical training is delivered onsite at the employer's facility, on its equipment and specimens. Atlantis does not run a public walk-in classroom.

Can the online hours count toward the ASNT NDT Level II?

ASNT sets its own training minimums for the central Level II, stated in days per method. Documented formal training can support your application. Check ASNT's current requirements when you apply.
