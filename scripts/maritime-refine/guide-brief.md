# Brief: maritime ship inspection guides for atlantisndt.com (2026-11-23)

Repo for this work: **the repository checkout** . Do NOT run git, do NOT run the site build, do NOT edit any file except the guide JSON files you are assigned (`src/data/maritime-section/pages/{slug}.json`).

## Why these pages exist
The owner (Atlantis NDT, a marine NDT and thickness measurement company) asked for "strategic content on how various vessels have to be inspected, what is special survey for vessels of different ages as per IACS recommended inspection practices", every page **2,000+ words of high-quality content**, to win the attention of ship owners, technical managers and superintendents. Search data from the site's first ten maritime days: topic pages earn about four times what port pages earn, so these guides are the highest-value pages in this batch. Write the page an experienced marine surveyor or superintendent would bookmark: accurate, specific, practical, honest about what class decides.

The guides are reference articles (Article schema), not sales pages. They explain how the survey or the ship type works, what the owner must prepare, where steel and cracks are usually found, and how the owner can get ahead of the class survey. Atlantis appears only where it naturally helps the reader (owner's pre-survey gauging, CVIP, pre-purchase and condition surveys), never as the main subject.

## Page model
Each guide lives at `/maritime/{slug}` and is rendered from `src/data/maritime-section/pages/{slug}.json` by `textPage()` (kind 'service', group 'guide') in `src/data/maritime-section/model.mjs` and `src/data/service-section/core.mjs`. The template adds breadcrumbs, the hero (title/h1/intro/highlights), your sections, codes, deliverables ("What the owner should have ready" works for guides), industries (ship types), FAQs, related links (the other guides and the owner services) and the enquiry band. Read `pages/enhanced-survey-programme.json` and `pages/special-survey-thickness-measurement.json` first: they show the JSON shape and depth, and **your guide must not repeat their sentences**. Also skim `pages/condition-assessment-scheme.json`, `pages/condition-assessment-programme.json`, `pages/hull-structural-integrity.json` and `pages/comprehensive-vessel-inspection-programme.json` so you link to them by name and do not duplicate them. `SERVICE_DEFS` in model.mjs lists every maritime slug and label you may link to (`/maritime/{slug}`).

## JSON fields
`slug`, `navLabel` (as in SERVICE_DEFS), `title` (max 65 chars, unique, the searcher's words first, e.g. "Bulk Carrier Inspection: Holds, Hatch Covers and ESP Surveys"), `description` (120-160 chars, count them), `h1` (different wording from the title), `eyebrow` ("Ship inspection guide"), `datePublished` ("2026-11-23"), `intro` (70-100 words), `highlights` (exactly 3 `{h, p}`, p 35-55 words), `sections` (8-11 objects `{ "id": "kebab-id", "h": "query-shaped heading", "p": ["paragraph", ...], "list"?: [...], "links"?: [["/maritime/slug", "label"], ...], "table"?: { "head": [...], "rows": [[...], ...] } }`), `codes` (6-10 rules and standards, written out correctly with their real titles), `deliverables` (5-7 items: for guides, what the owner should have ready or what a good survey record contains), `industries` (5-8 ship types or readers), `faqs` (6-8 `{q, a}`, answers 50-100 words, phrased as people search).

**Length: 1,900-2,500 words of your own copy** (intro + highlights + sections incl. lists and tables + FAQs), so the guide itself carries 2,000 words of substance; the rendered page will then be well over 2,000. At least two tables per guide (e.g. survey number vs what is examined; space vs typical defect; age vs coating consequence).

## Technical accuracy (most important)
These guides cite IACS Unified Requirements and Recommendations, the 2011 ESP Code (IMO Res. A.1049(27), mandatory through SOLAS XI-1/2), SOLAS chapters, MARPOL, the IGC and IBC Codes. Rules:
- **Verify before you state.** Research the IACS documents themselves where you can (iacs.org.uk publishes the URs and Recommendations as PDFs; WebFetch the PDF links your searches return). If you cannot verify a number (an age band, a percentage, an interval, a UR or Rec number or title), describe the requirement in words without the number. A wrong number is worse than no number.
- Leads to verify (do NOT copy them unchecked): hull survey cycle (annual survey window, intermediate in place of the second or third annual, renewal survey every five years, two bottom examinations in each five-year period with a maximum interval between them); renewal survey numbering by age (No. 1 up to 5 years, No. 2 over 5 up to 10, No. 3 over 10 up to 15, No. 4 and later over 15) and how close-up survey and thickness measurement extents grow with each in the annexes of UR Z10.1/Z10.2/Z10.3/Z10.4/Z10.5 (ESP ships) and UR Z7 / Z7.1 (other dry cargo ships), UR Z7.2 for liquefied gas carriers; the intermediate survey of older ESP ships taking on more of the preceding renewal survey's scope; coating condition GOOD / FAIR / POOR and what POOR or soft coating triggers (annual examination of ballast tanks); substantial and excessive corrosion; the survey planning questionnaire, survey programme, survey planning meeting and survey report file under the ESP Code; IACS Recommendations such as Rec. 76 (bulk carriers: guidelines for surveys, assessment and repair of hull structure), Rec. 96 (double hull oil tankers), Rec. 55 (general cargo ships), Rec. 84 (container ships), Rec. 87 (coating maintenance in ballast tanks) — confirm each number and title before citing it; UR S31 (side shell frame renewal criteria, single side skin bulk carriers), UR S21 (hatch covers and coamings); UR S8 / S9 (bow doors, side and stern doors); container ship hatch-corner and coaming cracking and the large-ship rules on thick plating and crack arrest; IGC Code cargo containment types and hold space inspection; IBC Code for chemical tankers.
- Class decides. The extent of any survey is set by the class society's rules and the attending surveyor; say so where it matters. Never present Atlantis as able to grant, credit or replace any class or statutory survey.
- Keep figures to what you verified, and name the source document in the text where it helps ("the annex tables of UR Z10.2 set...").
