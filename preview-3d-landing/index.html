Five inspection methods. One certified team.

From phased-array UT on critical pipework to digital RT on welded assemblies, we cover the full surface and volumetric inspection stack.

UT · 01
Ultrasonic Testing
Conventional, phased-array, and TOFD ultrasonic for weld integrity, thickness mapping, and corrosion mapping.
RT · 02
Radiographic Testing
Computed and digital radiography on pressure vessels and structural welds — DICONDE-compliant deliverables.

Wet and dry MT for surface and near-surface flaws on ferromagnetic welds, castings, and forgings.

Direct and remote VT with borescopes, drones, and ROV — full photographic and video evidence packages.
