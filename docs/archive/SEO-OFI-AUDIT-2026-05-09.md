### OFI-2 — Old salary URLs still showing (HIGH)
**Status:** Open.

Two URLs both targeted by vercel.json 301 redirects to `/blog/ndt-salary-guide-2026-global` are STILL ranking and bleeding impressions:
- `/blog/ndt-salary-guide-2025-global-level-1-2-3` — 918 impr, pos 5.4, 0.22% CTR
- `/blog/ndt-technician-salary-guide-2026-industry-report` — 919 impr, pos 5.4, 0.33% CTR

Combined: ~1,840 impressions/month, ~5 clicks total, ~115 lost vs expected CTR.

**Root cause:** these slugs ARE in `src/data/blogs.json` so prerender keeps regenerating them in dist/, shadowing the vercel.json redirects (Vercel filesystem precedence).

**Fix path A (recommended):** remove these 2 entries from `src/data/blogs.json`. Prerender stops generating dist HTML. Vercel.json 301 fires. Google updates cached URL within 7-14 days of next crawl.

**Fix path B (faster):** keep blogs.json entries but add `noindex` meta to those 2 routes via prerender override. Lets the redirect fire while telling Google to drop them.
