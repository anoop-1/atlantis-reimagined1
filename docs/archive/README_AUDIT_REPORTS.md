## KEY FINDINGS (SUMMARY)

### ✅ STRENGTHS
- 239 location pages with strong template infrastructure
- Consulting location pages (96) are 1,200-1,500 words with unique, location-specific content
- Hreflang support built into SEOHead component (not currently used)
- 5 satellite sites with 328 backlinks providing foundation
- Diverse geographic coverage across 7 regions

### 🔴 CRITICAL ISSUES
1. **Hreflang Tags NOT Implemented** — Causing regional cannibalization, losing ~20% potential traffic
2. **Only 72 Method+Location Pages** — Missing 168 pages across major cities (Mumbai, Bangkok, Sydney, Lagos, etc.)
3. **Insufficient Backlinks** — 1.4 backlinks/page average (need 5-20 for top 3 rankings)
4. **Geographic Imbalance** — APAC has only 36 pages for 1.5B people; Africa has 5% coverage
5. **Keyword Gaps** — Missing industry-specific applications: weld inspection, corrosion mapping, phased array, shipbuilding, etc.

### ⚠️ MODERATE CONCERNS
- Digital Twin pages (21) only covering 21 cities — should be 50+
- ERP pages (21) same gap
- No local GMB presence established
- Limited case studies/testimonials visible
- Backlinks not concentrated on high-priority pages
