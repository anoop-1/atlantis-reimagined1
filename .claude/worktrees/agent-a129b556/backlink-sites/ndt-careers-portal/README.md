# NDT Careers Portal - Satellite Site #5

A complete Next.js 14 satellite site focused on NDT careers, salary information, and job market analysis. This site drives traffic to Atlantis NDT's consulting pages and location-specific pages through strategic content and natural backlinks.

## Project Overview

**Site Focus:** NDT Careers, Consulting, and Salary Information  
**Target Audience:** NDT professionals, career changers, employers seeking NDT services  
**Primary Purpose:** Drive traffic to atlantisndt.com consulting and career pages  
**Technology:** Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS (Sky/Cyan theme)
