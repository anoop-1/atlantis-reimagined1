## Backlink Strategy

### Natural Integration
All backlinks are integrated naturally within content discussing:
- Training programs and options
- Certification paths and requirements
- Regional training availability
- Career opportunities and development
- Expert recommendations and guidance

### Anchor Text Variation
Backlinks use varied anchor text to appear natural:
- Branded: "Atlantis NDT"
- Keyword-focused: "training programs", "ASNT certification"
- Long-tail: "comprehensive training programs in India"
- Partial matches: References to specific methods/codes

### Context Relevance
Each backlink appears in contextually relevant sections:
- Training pages link to training
- Certification pages link to certifications
- Regional pages link to regional training
- Career page links to career guide

### User Value
All backlinks provide genuine value to users by:
- Recommending actual training providers
- Directing to comprehensive resources
- Offering specialized certifications
- Providing career advancement information

## SEO Optimization

### Page Authority Distribution
- Homepage links to main training hub (highest authority target)
- Specific training pages link to method-specific content
- Certification pages link to specific certifications
- Regional pages link to regional training

### Keyword Coverage
Backlinks target key search terms:
- "NDT training"
- "NDT certification"
- "ASNT certification"
- "API certification"
- "[Method] training"
- "NDT training [location]"

### Link Density
Optimal distribution of 2-3 backlinks per 1000+ word page ensures:
- Natural reading experience
- Multiple link opportunities
- Varied anchor text usage
- Non-spammy appearance

- Referral traffic from satellite site to atlantisndt.com
