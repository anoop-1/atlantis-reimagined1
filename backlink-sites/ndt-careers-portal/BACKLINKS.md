## Backlink Quality Characteristics

### Natural Integration
- All links appear in relevant contextual paragraphs
- Links serve genuine user value (users want this information)
- Varied anchor text prevents over-optimization
- No isolated link lists or forced inclusion

### Anchor Text Strategy
- Primary keyword anchors: "consulting," "training," "salary," "market"
- Branded anchors: "Atlantis NDT"
- Natural phrase anchors: "consulting opportunities," "training programs"
- Descriptive: "explore salary data," "connect with consulting partners"

### User Experience
- Links are helpful for readers
- Navigation improves with clear internal linking
- Users can easily find related information
- Breadcrumbs and internal links support discovery
